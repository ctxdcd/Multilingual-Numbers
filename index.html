A LITTLE GUIDE

About Numbers.

A simple way to explore, learn and remember numbers across seven different languages.

01 — THE COLLECTION

Seven languages.
One learning space.

Explore numbers in Japanese, Russian, German, French, Turkish, Kurdish (Kurmanji) and Arabic.

02 — HOW IT WORKS

Start with the basics.

1
Choose a language

Select one of the seven available languages. Japanese also offers Hiragana and Kanji.

2
Pick a category

Start small with 0–10, then gradually explore larger number ranges.

3
Explore and recall

Tap a number to see its written form and pronunciation, then test your memory.

4
Keep practising

Choose between four answers, or switch to Typing Mode and write the answer yourself. Your True and False counters track your progress.

03 — A SMALL REMINDER

One category a day.

You don't have to memorise everything at once. Take one category, practise until it sticks, and move on when you're ready.

GOOD TO KNOW

Practice is completely self-paced. There are no time limits or question limits. Keep going for as long as you like, and leave whenever you're ready. Use the round arrow to exit. Your selected language stays locked during practice.

Large numbers may have different naming systems across languages. For example, German Milliarde means one billion.
