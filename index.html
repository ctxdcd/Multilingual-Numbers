Numbers

About

A small personal project for practising numbers in seven languages: Japanese, Russian, German, French, Turkish, Kurdish (Kurmanji) and Arabic.

How to use

  1. Pick a language with the chips at the top. For Japanese you can also choose Hiragana or Kanji.
  2. Choose a scale, such as 0–10 or Millions+.
  3. Tap a number to see its written form and pronunciation.
  4. Start practising from a single number or a whole scale.
  5. Choose the right answer out of A, B, C and D. The True and False counters keep score.
  6. Keep going as long as you like. Practice never ends by itself. Press the round arrow to leave.

Good to know

The language is locked during practice, so exit first if you want to switch. Big numbers are named differently in each language, for example a German "Milliarde" is a billion.
